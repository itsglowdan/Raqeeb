# Presentation content draft

1. **Problem and users:** water-network supervisors need understandable assessment and task coordination. Explain the educational scope.
2. **Data:** show one original record, reference, exact coordinates, units and dataset label. Explain missing measurement time and pipeline geometry.
3. **Architecture:** explain approved five roles, two team instances and ordinary validation/recording functions. Show the interaction diagram.
4. **Rules:** pressure and flow are independently checked. Explain Normal, Suspected and dual-rule assessment. Distinguish configurable rules from engineering safety standards.
5. **Live demonstration:** prepare a custom sequence, Run, inspect actual messages, Pause and Resume. Do not present expected outcomes as observed results.
6. **Coordination and failure:** demonstrate approval or team failure, reassignment and an explicit terminal outcome. Download the actual message log and capture observed outcomes.
7. **Evidence:** show the team's three saved manual experiment results and one development test result. Explain limitations of evaluation on the same dataset.
8. **Business model and next steps:** summarize target users, value, delivery, potential revenue and future operational validation. Avoid claiming existing customers or proven savings.
